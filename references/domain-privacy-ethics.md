# Domain knowledge, privacy, security, ethics, and sustainability

Operational guide and topic inventory. Procedures and checks are an original synthesis; use the source register for the authority, inspected portion, and limits of specific references.

### Coverage

| Area | Details to consider |
| --- | --- |
| Domain model | Roles; user/buyer/operator; entities; relationships; terminology; field meaning; calculations; lifecycle; operational constraints. |
| Critical workflows | Eligibility; authorization; financial/clinical/physical consequences; approvals; exceptions; audit; handoff; recovery; escalation. |
| Privacy | Purpose; data minimization; consent where applicable; access; visibility; masking; sharing; retention; deletion; exports; delegation. |
| Security UX | Authentication; sessions; recovery; devices; permission scope; secure defaults; understandable warnings; abuse reporting; social threats. |
| Ethics | Agency; informed choice; coercion; misleading defaults; manipulation; vulnerable users; dignity; exclusion; incentives; unintended harm. |

### Procedure

1. Build a domain profile from verified sources and actual stakeholders. Record meaning before designing fields and actions.
2. Identify critical tasks, consequences, users, environments, and the specialist responsibilities attached to them.
3. Specify privacy and security behavior with accountable owners, including access, sharing, recovery, and loss of authorization.
4. Review incentives and choices for user agency, exclusion, coercion, and unintended burden.
5. Design reporting, appeal, support, and recovery where product misuse or harm is plausible and relevant.
6. Assess actual resource and operational costs before making sustainability claims.
7. Verify applicable requirements and source versions for the task; involve appropriate qualified review for consequential domains.
8. Record evidence, conflicts, residual limitations, and triggers for re-evaluation.

### Outputs

Domain glossary/model; critical-task register; trust/privacy/security patterns; applicability and specialist-review record; ethical assessment.

### Quality checks

Business and data meaning are verified; user agency is clear; sensitive information is appropriately handled; safety and legal claims stay within available evidence; consequences and recovery are specified.

### Limits and common failures

A generic domain pack cannot certify a medical device, legal service, financial product, or industrial interface. Public standard abstracts do not supply the full standard. Existing domain starter packs are scope inventories; deepen them with current authoritative and organizational information for each real task.
